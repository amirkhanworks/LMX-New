Our story

Luminox began with Fahad Khan, an electrical engineer who wanted to bring real smart home automation to Indian homes without the cost and hassle of rewiring. Saad Khan joined early to shape the product's design. Amir Khan came on board to lead technology and business. Today the three of them are building both the smart home product and Luminox's engineering services.

Founded 2020 · Patent-pending · 100+ on the Mumbai waiting list · Soft launching soon

Vision

To empower people with complete control over their homes through smart and reliable technology.

Mission

To make smart home automation affordable, easy to use, and accessible to everyone.
